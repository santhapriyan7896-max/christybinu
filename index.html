Christy Binu is a Kerala-based creative force living at the intersection of cinema, color science, and artistry. With a trained eye for visual storytelling and a deep mastery of post-production, Christy transforms raw footage into cinematic experience — frame by frame.

Operating under the production imprint @x.illume, Christy specializes in non-linear editing, Digital Intermediate (DI) color grading, and independent filmmaking. Every project is a canvas. Every grade, a voice.
